The two halves are usually asked for separately — `{% pager section="size" %}` above the list and `{% pager section="steps" %}` below it — because they are wanted at different moments: how long the page is before you read it, and how to leave the page after you have. `section="both"` renders one bar, which is what a list with nothing to step through wants. Links come from {% pagination_url %} / {% page_size_url %}, which rewrite one parameter of the real query string.
