{% comment %} Past its action deadline. A condition lying across the live stages rather than one of them: a document can be pending receipt and overdue at the same time, and a clerk needs both — the stage says what to do next, the tag says how urgent it is. It used to replace the stage. `display_status` returned "OVERDUE" whenever the deadline had passed, so a reader who opened the Pending receipt slice counted two records and one Pending receipt pill, and the exported CSV had no record at all of what stage the late documents were at. Derived from due_at, never stored. A completed record is never overdue: a deadline that passed before the work finished is not a debt anybody still owes. "Due soon" is the same idea one step earlier — approaching, and still actionable. Once a record is overdue the warning has arrived too late to be a warning, so the two are one control with two states rather than two tags: they are mutually exclusive by construction, and a row can never wear both.
