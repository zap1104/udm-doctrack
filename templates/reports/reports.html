Turnaround

Averages from the sent, received and completed timestamps, counted in office hours. Calendar time is shown beneath each one.
{% comment %} Both figures, always. Office hours say how much working time an office had to act, which is the fair way to judge an office — a document routed Friday 4PM and received Monday 9AM waited about an hour of anybody's attention. Calendar time is what the requester actually waited, which is the fair way to answer them. Either one alone misleads, in opposite directions. {% endcomment %}
