Averages use exact seconds; displayed hours and minutes are rounded down. ≈ means approximately equal. Excluded time varies with each document’s dates and times.

What counts: Pending Receipt measures each confirmed handover from sending to confirmation. One document can have several handovers. In Process measures each completed document from its first receipt to completion, including later transfers and waits across offices. Total lifetime measures creation to completion. These spans overlap and must not be added.

Which month: A handover belongs to the month it was confirmed; a document belongs to the month it was completed, even if it started earlier. Completed documents waiting for repository upload are included. Approval and upload time are excluded. Only documents you are permitted to view in the selected office scope contribute.

How the average works: Add the valid times within office hours and divide by the count shown for that stage, including observations with zero counted minutes. The monthly average uses all individual observations, not an average of the daily dots. Both clocks are displayed in total hours so they can be compared directly. {{ turnaround.office_hours_caveat }}

Zero and missing data: 0 mins means a valid interval with no counted working time, including instant confirmations or intervals outside office hours. These observations stay in the average and are excluded from Fastest. A dash means no valid observation. Missing or reversed timestamps and future confirmations or completions are excluded. Waiting handovers are a live count across all months and enter the average only after confirmation. Documents already Received or In Process can still await other recipients, so this count can exceed the Pending Receipt stage count.
