Tracking workload
The documents in the linked Tracking list for your permissions and office selection. It includes work completed but still awaiting filing where the list includes it.
Incoming and Outgoing
Unfinished documents in the current routing batch, addressed to or sent by the selected office. Outgoing includes documents already received. These are overlapping views, not parts of a total.
Overdue
Unfinished documents whose action deadline has passed. The card covers the scoped tracking list; the rings describe the current transfer. A document previously handled by the office can appear on the card without appearing in either ring.
Tracking queues
Browse tracking records by stage or direction. Use Confirm Receipt when a document has arrived at your office.
Latest tracking activity
Up to five tracking records with the most recent activity, within your permitted office scope. Activity includes creation, routing, receipt confirmation, remarks and status changes. Completed records awaiting filing can also appear.
Turnaround for the selected month
Pending Receipt measures sent to confirmed, per handover. In Process measures first receipt to completion, per document. Total lifetime measures creation to completion, per document. These spans overlap. A month uses all its samples, rather than averaging the daily averages. Blank dates have no samples.
Working and elapsed time
Working time uses configured office hours, lunch breaks, weekends and the administrator-maintained holiday calendar. Elapsed time is the full wait. These figures describe document timelines across offices, not individual staff performance.
Repository additions
Active repository documents created during the current calendar month, including historical uploads, scans and files from Tracking. Each repository document counts once. Completion alone does not add a repository document.

Open a tracking card to inspect its records. Use the separate Tracking and Repository Reports pages for charts and searchable record lists. Filters on a report's record list apply to that list only.
